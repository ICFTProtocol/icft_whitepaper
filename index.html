Executive Summary

ICFT is a decentralized lending and liquidity protocol built around a fixed-supply ERC-20 token. It combines collateralized lending, market liquidity and on-chain risk management.

Users deposit ETH as collateral and receive an open-ended ICFT loan. The economic obligation is denominated in USD: principal is fixed at origination in USD, while the ICFT amount required for repayment changes with the token price.

Lending Mechanics

Users deposit ETH and the oracle determines collateral value in USD. Maximum Debt = Collateral USD × 80%.

The loan is issued in ICFT while principal is fixed in USD at origination.

At ICFT $1, an 80,000 ICFT loan represents $80,000 principal. At ICFT $2, 40,000 ICFT is required for principal repayment; at $0.50, 160,000 ICFT is required.

ICFT price changes do not change the USD principal. The borrower repays the USD-equivalent principal plus accrued interest.

Repayment may be made in ICFT, USDT or ETH. If USDT/ETH is used, the protocol automatically purchases the required ICFT through an integrated DEX/on-chain market and returns it to the protocol. Interest remains protocol revenue.

LTV and Risk

LTV = Debt USD / Collateral USD × 100%. Maximum LTV is 80%. At 90%+ the position becomes eligible for partial liquidation.

Position health
0%80% max LTV90%+ liquidation100%

Only the amount necessary to restore LTV to ≤85% is liquidated. Fund B is not used to cover bad debt. It temporarily supports Fund A above 95% utilization and may later receive the transferred ICFT back.

07 / 22

Liquidation and Keeper

liquidate() is permissionless.

The protocol's own keeper monitors positions and may execute liquidations without receiving a separate reward. External liquidators may execute liquidate() and receive the liquidation bonus.

Liquidation bonus: 5%. A liquidator repaying $10,000 receives collateral worth $10,500; $500 is the liquidator bonus.

Collateral is sold for USDT through an integrated DEX/on-chain market. Liquidation bonus is not protocol revenue; lending interest is protocol revenue.

Liquidity & External LPs

Liquidity allocation: 150,000,000 ICFT (15%).

The protocol supplies ICFT while external LPs supply USDT. The 150M allocation is not a requirement to deploy the entire amount into one pool.

A temporary bootstrap incentive program is planned. Reward size and duration are set before mainnet based on actual liquidity needs. LPs receive trading fees; additional ICFT incentives are limited to the temporary program.

Vesting & Allocation Controls

The Founder and Developer allocations use two separate parameters: a 6-month cliff and a 24-month vesting period.

Cliff ≠ Vesting. The 6-month cliff is the period during which no tokens are unlocked. After the cliff, the 24-month vesting period begins and the allocation vests linearly.

Conclusion

ICFT combines fixed-supply ERC-20, collateralized lending, USD-denominated debt, utilization-based interest, partial liquidation, ICFT/USDT liquidity and multiple token-usage channels.

The core model is straightforward: users borrow ICFT against collateral, debt is accounted for in USD, returned principal replenishes lending liquidity, and interest generates protocol revenue.

The next stage is MVP implementation, testing, audit, liquidity preparation and mainnet launch.
